Solutions

Built for the people who sign off materials.

CertiSift fits between goods-in and production, where quality, procurement and the lab meet.

QA managers

Set the specifications once and get consistent release decisions from every reviewer, with a record for audits.

How they use it: Define each material's limits and the names suppliers use, then review held lots and sign off reports.

Incoming-goods QA

Check a certificate in the time it takes to open it, and see exactly which line needs attention.

How they use it: Paste or upload each certificate at goods-in and release, hold or reject with a note.

Supplier quality

See which suppliers drift, which parameters fail repeatedly, and back corrective-action requests with data.

How they use it: Use each supplier's pass rate, recurring failures and trends in supplier reviews.

Procurement

Bring quality history into supplier reviews and sourcing decisions, not just price and lead time.

How they use it: Compare suppliers of the same material on their certificate record before renewing contracts.

Starting with

Food and beverage manufacturers

Flour, dairy powders, cocoa, sugar, oils and other ingredients that arrive with a CoA on every lot and are tested for moisture, composition, contaminants and pathogens.
