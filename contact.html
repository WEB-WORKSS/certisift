Is CertiSift available today?

CertiSift is building its initial product. The live demo runs the verification engine in your browser on text-based certificates and fictional suppliers. Early-access requests are open for pilots with food and beverage QA teams.

Which documents can it read?

Today: certificates as plain text or CSV, pasted or uploaded. Scanned PDFs and images will be handled through Amazon Textract in the production platform.

What does the AI do?

In the planned platform, Amazon Textract reads scanned certificates and Amazon Bedrock interprets unfamiliar layouts and explains findings. Pass, warn and fail come from deterministic checks against your specification, not from the AI.

Does it replace our QA team's judgement?

No. CertiSift does the line-by-line comparison and highlights what needs attention. The release decision, and the note explaining it, stay with your reviewer.

Who can change what?

QA managers own specifications, reviewers record release decisions, and auditors get read-only access. In the demo roles are not enforced; in production they are enforced on the server.

Can we use our own specifications?

Yes. Specifications are set up per material with ranges, minimums, maximums, pathogen requirements and the parameter names your suppliers use.

How much does it cost?

Proposed launch pricing starts at $149 a month for one site. Final pricing is set with pilot customers, and no payment is taken today. See the Pricing page.

Where is our data stored?

In the demo, everything stays in your own browser's local storage. The production platform is designed for encrypted storage on AWS, with customer data separated by tenant.
