Roadmap

Where CertiSift is going

Built with early-access QA teams. The order is our plan and may change based on what pilots teach us.

  1. Now

    Live demo & early access

    • Verification engine: extraction, matching and anomaly checks
    • Interactive live demo with sample suppliers
    • Proposed AWS architecture defined
    • Early-access requests open
  2. Next

    Pilot on one site

    • Your own specifications and suppliers
    • Scanned PDF and image extraction with Amazon Textract
    • Bedrock-assisted interpretation of unfamiliar layouts
  3. Then

    General availability

    • Team workspaces with roles and approval steps
    • Email-in: forward supplier CoAs to a workspace inbox
    • Pricing confirmed with pilot customers
  4. Later

    Exploring with pilot customers

    • ERP / LIMS integration for automatic hold and release
    • Chemical and pharmaceutical manufacturers (needs GxP work)
    • Supplier portal for corrected certificates
