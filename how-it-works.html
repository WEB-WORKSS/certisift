How it works

Four steps, one record.

The same workflow for every certificate, whichever supplier or lab produced it.

Receive

Paste or upload the supplier's certificate and choose the material specification.

.txt · .csv · pasted text

Extract

Supplier, product, lot, dates, signatory and every result with its unit are pulled out and normalised.

3.8 × 10⁴ → 38,000 cfu/g

Check

Each result is compared with your limits and with the supplier's previous lots.

limits · completeness · history

Decide

Release, hold or reject with a note. The report and supplier record update automatically.

accept · hold · reject
AI, used carefully

The rules decide. The AI reads and explains.

Pass, warn and fail come from your specification and deterministic checks you can trace. AI is used to read difficult documents and explain findings, and your reviewer makes every release decision.

What the AI does

  • Reads scanned PDFs and images (planned, with Amazon Textract)
  • Interprets unfamiliar certificate layouts and maps parameter names (planned, with Amazon Bedrock)
  • Explains why a lot was flagged and lists the checks behind it
  • Suggests a note for the reviewer to edit

What it never does

  • Release, hold or reject a lot
  • Change your specification limits
  • Fill in a missing test result
  • Use one customer's certificates to answer another's questions

Why was lot AGP-7781-B flagged?

Two findings. Aflatoxin B1 is 3.4 µg/kg against your maximum of 2.0 µg/kg, so it fails the Wheat Flour Type 550 specification. Moisture is 14.4% against a 14.5% maximum, which passes but is within the near-limit band.

From: certificate AGP-7781-B · spec Wheat Flour Type 550 v3

That's your reviewer's decision. I can check the new certificate against the same specification and show what changed.
