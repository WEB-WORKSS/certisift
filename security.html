Security

Security and safeguards

How CertiSift is designed to protect supplier certificates, keep reviewers in control and keep every verdict traceable, and what is and isn't built yet.

What the demo does with data

  • The live demo runs entirely in your browser on fictional suppliers and certificates.
  • Certificates and specifications you add are stored in your browser's local storage only and never sent to a server.
  • You can reset the demo data at any time from Settings.

How production will handle certificates

  • Certificates, specifications and results are stored on AWS, partitioned by customer.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with customer-managed keys.
  • Hosting regions are agreed with each pilot customer.

Access and roles

  • Users sign in with Amazon Cognito; there are no shared logins.
  • Reviewers, QA managers and auditors each get only the actions their role needs.
  • Roles are enforced on the server, not only in the interface.

How the AI is constrained

  • Pass, warn and fail come from deterministic checks against your specification.
  • AI only reads documents, maps unfamiliar layouts and explains findings.
  • Release decisions are always recorded by a named reviewer.

Audit trail

  • Every verification keeps the original certificate text, each check, the verdict and the reviewer's note.
  • Reports and CSV exports are available for audits.

What isn't built yet

  • The AWS document pipeline, sign-in, server-side roles and multi-user approvals are proposed, not deployed.
  • No certifications are claimed, and the product is not validated for 21 CFR Part 11.
Safeguards

Built for QA teams that have to sign the release.

Every verdict is traceable

Each result shows the extracted value, the specification limit and the check that produced pass, warn or fail.

People make the call

The release decision and the note behind it always belong to a named reviewer.

Missing is never a pass

A test that the specification requires but the certificate lacks is flagged as missing.

Supplier data kept separate

The architecture isolates each customer's certificates and encrypts them with customer-managed keys.

Roles, enforced

Reviewers, QA managers and auditors each get only the actions they need. Production enforces roles on the server.

Everything recorded

Verifications, decisions and specification changes are kept in the workspace record and exportable for audits.
